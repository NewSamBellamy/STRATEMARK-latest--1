# Keystone delivery map — keep the whole product in view

Updated 2026-10-05. This is the current execution map, not a replacement product vision or a claim of production readiness. Read the consolidated build/test plan and architecture specs referenced in KEYSTONE-BUILD-STATUS first. Preserve the approved collectible card visual baseline and separate revival branch.

## What progress means

A slice must improve an actual connected user action, retain the result locally, and have a reproducible verification. A scaffolding file, passing fixture suite, source count or generated Supported label is not a shipped capability. Track fixture results, live provider results and packaging results separately. No more sequential integrity checkpoints should be mistaken for completion of the entire product.

## Nine delivery areas

1. **Trustworthy first-ready research.** Existing protected numeric gates, original retention and targeted excerpts are partial foundations. Finish native/source access evaluation, company versus subsidiary identity, reporting periods and metric definitions, accepted-fact history, conflicts and source priority. Exit: a small public/private-company run yields inspectable accepted figures or honest specific unknowns, with measured first-ready timing and request counts; card and reader agree on the evidence.
2. **Deck Sentinel and persistent Card Scouts.** Existing open-deck runtime is not the finished architecture. Complete bounded discovery, deduplication, company/infrastructure/distribution assignment, stable scout identity, resumable work records, prompt/version tracking, retries, cancellation and per-capability spend controls. Exit: interruption/reopen resumes the right company tasks without duplicate spend or false completeness. Initial card and reader evidence comes before broad dashboard enrichment.
3. **Company dossier and all dashboard sections.** Existing screens/queries are not proof of high-quality content. Complete evidence-backed business overview, figures, products, live developments, history, governance and team. Accurately distinguish parent/subsidiary/member identities. Exit: each visible tab has useful sourced content or a specific, actionable unavailable state; canonical corrections flow to all views. No fake trend points or headshots.
4. **Branding and collectible-card finish.** Preserve current proportions, hero size, compact metrics and designer palette. Audit domain/logo resolution, retry and fallbacks, brand-color attribution, executive photos, one-line copy, overflow and reader interactions. Exit: approved small/public/company and four finding-card examples look cohesive at desktop/mobile sizes and keyboard navigation works. No broad unsolicited redesign.
5. **Specialist finding cards and reports.** Insight, Culture, Vice and Barrier to Entry have dedicated market-level research, not inherited company figures. Finish sourced findings, concise face copy, story reader, read-more report, optional chat and cited PDF/export. Exit: a real finding goes from discovery to readable report to sharing/reopen with citations intact; unsupported adverse allegations never publish. Entity comparison/briefing actions must remain appropriate to card type.
6. **Ranking and meaningful monitoring.** Proposed scoring is not implementation. Finish deterministic evidence-aware cohort scoring, unknown coverage, comparable bases, explanations, reranking on membership changes, subtle legend and distinction from investment quality. Monitoring must produce material change events, not periodic prose churn. Exit: adding/removing a company recomputes ranks reproducibly; opt-in scheduled work respects pause/cadence and surfaces a useful evidence-backed change.
7. **Any-key capability routing.** Gemini is the present path, not universal-provider coverage. Separate language/synthesis, web search/grounding/retrieval, and image capabilities. Add adapters incrementally with explicit supported/unsupported features, key-local storage, model selection, fallback consent and real smoke tests. Exit: at least one non-Gemini stack completes the same sourced company journey; mixed stacks improve a measured outcome rather than just adding toggles. Do not assume paid ChatGPT credits or OAuth model access is available without supported provider terms and implementation.
8. **Local vault, sharing and agent actions.** Browser snapshots and separate native originals are not a finished queryable dossier system. Complete indexed company/claim/source artifacts, migrations, native artifact backup/export/import/deletion, recovery, search and history. Define stable application actions (scope research, get dossier, verify metric, read evidence, refresh, pause, export) before exposing MCP. Exit: another agent can use authorized scoped actions against local data with progress, idempotency, cancellation and consent; it cannot read credentials or arbitrary files. Share one card/deck cleanly, with explicit redaction and preserved evidence.
9. **Production release and complete user journey.** Test install, BYOK onboarding, discover, inspect, deeper research, corrections, save/reopen, reports, share/import, updates and uninstall/recovery. Finish security boundaries, prompt-injection handling, accessibility, responsive states, source/asset licensing, crash recovery and provider-error handling. Exit: release-candidate installer and recorded real journey pass, no dead controls, no silent loss/spend, and known limitations are documented. Unit tests or an HTTP-200 preview alone are not this gate.

## Every checkpoint

- Record changed user-visible behavior, backend wiring, tests and actual live/packaging validation.
- Ask: did this improve research usefulness, time to first useful card, continuity, trust, or an actual interaction? If not, stop adding architecture and choose a connected vertical slice.
- Record uncovered risks and the next measurable acceptance gate, not just the next file to edit.
- Preserve user data and approved visuals; no hidden key copying, unlimited research, main merge or unverified remote-backup claim.
- Keep plans and handoffs concise and linked to durable artifacts. Budget and context-efficient execution remain important; this map does not authorize broad paid runs.
