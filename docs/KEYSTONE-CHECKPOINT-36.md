## Red team / deliberately unfinished

SEC disclosure is source-reported, not independent truth/audit; mechanical support does not guarantee all business claims. Only this standard US-GAAP USD revenue concept and exact whole-issuer legal names are supported. Banks, foreign/custom taxonomies, alternative revenue concepts, aliases, private-company figures and bodies over the limit need additional evaluated adapters. No fabricated replacement metrics. Source failure remains unavailable/unknown. Two original reads per acquisition remain the existing budget; converting a filing to the concept can sacrifice other filing contents and has no extra hidden fallback request.

Native/browser distinction remains important: SEC disallows browser CORS. This does not make a direct browser BYOK SEC fetch universally work. The declared User-Agent and conservative per-process two-requests-per-second spacing are not an aggregate multi-instance cloud traffic controller; production SEC contact/fair-access configuration must be reviewed before scale. See [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) and [developer resources](https://www.sec.gov/about/developer-resources).

Indexed/pinned evidence retention beyond the current bounded window, chronological stored observation selection, narrative acceptance across all deeper dashboards, alias/legal-entity resolution and private-company coverage remain open. The shared `arr` slot cannot hold simultaneous ARR and annual-revenue facts; it is a compatibility compromise, not the final claim ledger. No key access, user-vault mutation, GitHub push, deployment or main merge. No full milestone or production-readiness claim.
