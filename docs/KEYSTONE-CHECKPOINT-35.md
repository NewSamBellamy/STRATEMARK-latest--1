Date: 2026-10-05 local. Branch: `revival/initial-card-redesign`. Parent: `bd2e406`.

## Connected improvement

Original passage support now retains an optional typed measurement definition and annual reporting start. The old `arr` and `users` keys remain storage/profile slots, not permission to conflate measurements. Supported definitions distinguish ARR from annual revenue and generic users from active, monthly/daily active users, customers and paying customers. No numeric migration or invented dates/bases were performed.

First company hydration, local/cloud verification and targeted hunts receive the same definition instructions. Annual revenue requires literal original USD support and a matching explicitly written annual interval. Scoped populations require an exact matching definition. Unsupported proxies, run-rate conversions, mismatched populations, explicit divisions/segments, estimates and missing evidence remain unknown. Existing publisher, original integrity, attribution and date checks remain mandatory. Untyped ARR/simple users retain compatibility; untyped scoped populations no longer publish as generic users.

Typed labels reach existing card/reader metrics and overview figures; overviews preserve reporting intervals. No card layout, colors, proportions or component structure changed. Annual revenue and scoped customer/active-user populations remain useful profile facts but are excluded from existing ARR/total-user chart axes and maturity scoring/cohort inputs because those interfaces cannot express comparable bases. This is not a new scoring model or a completed revenue chart.

Equal numbers with different definitions/reporting periods are no longer silently treated as agreeing in tied revisions or reconciliation. Conflicts retain each original passage support through schema parsing. A supported changed definition/known period invalidates interpretations even when the numeric value is unchanged. First attachment of proof to an unchanged legacy measurement preserves historical capture dates and zero-value behavior. Human corrections remain protected.

## Limits and next acceptance gate

This is a conservative literal-paragraph lane. Normal issuer first-person prose, tables, fiscal-year-only statements, currencies other than USD, approximate counts and most real filing formats remain unsupported. The interval grammar is intentionally narrow: it cannot invent the required phrase or dates from a table. Declared issuer website/whole-company metadata is not independent legal identity verification. Publication remains source-reported mechanical support, not semantic proof.

Reporting-date preference across multiple historical revisions, retrospective correction policy, indexed/pinned original retention beyond the current bounded window, meaningful complete ranking, and coverage-aware cohort selection still need work. Raw legacy history and deeper generated prose are not universally certified by this contract. A safer unknown is not sufficient product usefulness.

Next: carry one real regulator/issuer structured financial observation (entity, original artifact, actual measurement, currency and reporting interval) into this shared fact contract, with an independently inspectable fixture captured from a public original and an actual bounded provider journey when native controls are usable. Compare accepted coverage/first-ready latency, not merely test counts. Do not further grow regex heuristics to mimic table understanding. Keep the other nine delivery areas visible; the full backend goal remains active.
