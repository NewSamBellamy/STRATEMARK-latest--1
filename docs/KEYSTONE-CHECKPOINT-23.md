## Root cause fixed

The broad metric hunt did not use the configured original-source services at all. It accepted all model-proposed figures whenever the pass contained a verification-grade citation. A regression proved it promoted employees despite an unavailable original. Another proved it promoted unrelated/unsupported valuation and market-cap proposals alongside one supported employee passage. An in-flight human correction was also overwritten by the old write loop.

The existing hunt now reuses the two-source selector, scoped retrieval and acknowledged evidence retention. It stores the attempt before synthesis, skips synthesis entirely when neither original is readable, and accepts each figure through the existing exact original-passage/company/date/basis/unit gate. Final publisher citations and reporting-date notes belong to the accepted figure, not the whole search pass. Scoped grounded notes also persist for later company queries. The write loop rechecks hard/human-verified rows after provider work, and uses the existing valid-value guard.

No-reader integrations retain their legacy citation-only behavior and are explicitly still a coverage gap. Browser, native and any callers supplying original services take the protected path. No extra model fan-out, new provider, proxy, cache, source extractor or persistence schema. Research-output schema adds nullable passage support per hunt figure. A whole-company excerpt with a two-page budget can miss figures; do not claim exhaustive coverage.

The post-fix screenshot exposed a separate responsive bug: a long hunt outcome and research controls squeezed the headline KPI band into overlapping text. Headline metrics now own a full-width labeled region; actions wrap on a separate row. Existing colors, typography and collectible card design are unchanged. A semantic regression first failed on the missing region, then passed after the change; jsdom does not prove pixel layout.
