## What wins

A card opens a fast, trustworthy company dossier. Evidence survives locally, every fact has a meaning and reporting period, and useful changes bring the researcher back. Agents own durable assignments and bounded jobs; they do not burn credits forever.

## Active next category

Phase A: one publication standard for company metrics and their evidence. Fix weak verification, false freshness, metric definitions and period-aware reconciliation. Ship one complete Gemini card-and-reader slice with adversarial tests before expanding.

Then durable evidence storage; real scout/readiness jobs; specialist findings and shards; broad provider combinations; useful monitoring; MCP and release validation. Preserve the approved front-end design throughout.

## Handoff rules

- Read the specification and inspect the working tree before continuing.
- Distinguish implemented work from proposals. Never claim all company data can be guaranteed accurate or every key supports every capability.
- Check the known failing dashboard journey test; prior unit counts do not demonstrate live provider accuracy.
- Keep unrelated files and earlier experiments intact. No main merge or new cloud service by default.
- No paid research benchmarks until their scope and spend are approved.
- At each slice, record visible outcome, tests actually run, evidence quality, cost if known, risks and the next task.
