Legacy notes remain on disk; their unchecked financial prose is withheld with an explicit refresh explanation. There is no silent paid migration or data deletion.

## Remaining acceptance gates — do not claim bulletproof

- Both real browser refreshes withheld unsupported financial prose but produced no eligible background or accepted figures. Source coverage is not solved simply by returning Unknown. Inspection of `original-source.browser.ts` explains a critical capability gap: browser retrieval deliberately permits only a fixed set of established public hosts, omits arbitrary company domains such as OpenAI, requires CORS and forbids redirects. The new website fallback therefore needs protected desktop/configured retrieval; this preview cannot prove it succeeds. Do not weaken those protections or fabricate source content. A live useful protected-host journey and an explicit source-capability/unavailable explanation remain higher value than more paid browser retries.
- The live dashboard's separate **At a glance** panel still shows legacy estimates and a 62.5% market-share verification badge with no visible denominator/period proof. Other saved company cards also contain legacy figures. This checkpoint does not repair those read projections or prove across-surface consistency for legacy data.

Official URLs still require identity validation. The conservative English qualitative exclusion can omit useful version names/phrases and is not universal numerical/semantic detection across languages.
